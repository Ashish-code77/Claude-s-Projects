Before you start

Questions

Still unsure? Write to a specialist. They reply within one working day.

Do I need surgery or fillers?

No. Facelyne only suggests non-surgical changes: hair, grooming, brows, optional cosmetics and routines. You decide which changes you're open to before we start.

Who actually reviews my photos?

A named specialist with salon or barbershop experience. Software checks your photo quality and maps the angles; the looks are written by a person and checked by a second specialist.

What happens to my photos?

They're stored privately and used only to create your report and previews. You can delete them anytime from Settings, and they're never shown publicly without your written permission.

What if I don't like any of the five looks?

Tell your specialist what felt off and they'll redo the set for free.

How fast do I get results?

Within 72 hours as standard, or 24 hours with Express delivery at checkout.

Is this medical advice?

No. Facelyne offers style guidance and general routine suggestions. Speak to a qualified professional about medical or dental concerns.
